About Us

This portfolio is a record of the things we actually shipped.

Everything you scrolled past above started as a problem worth solving, not a demo. VoidShare came out of wanting file sharing where the server never sees your data. AI-WAF and the Cyber Tools suite grew from real traffic logs and the attack patterns hiding inside them. Agri-Mitra pushed us into blockchain because supply-chain trust can't be bolted on later. Life Replay, AI Object Tracking and the AI Wrapper Platform are where we took machine learning out of notebooks and into something a person can actually open and use.

Across those builds the work runs end to end — research and architecture, backend and APIs, model training, security hardening, interface and motion design, then deployment and the unglamorous maintenance that follows. Some of it is solo work; most of it is a small team splitting the hard parts and reviewing each other's code until it holds up.

What ties the projects together is a bias toward finishing: intelligent where intelligence earns its place, secure by default, and fast enough that nobody has to wait on it. Below are the people behind that work.
